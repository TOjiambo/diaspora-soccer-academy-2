Heart & Passion
Our Story

From Grassroots
To Greatness

Founded in the heart of Ongata Rongai, Kajiado County, Diaspora Soccer Academy — affectionately known as The Paws 🐾 — was born from a simple but powerful vision: to give every child, regardless of background, the chance to play, grow and dream big.

We believe that football is more than a game. It's a pathway to discipline, teamwork, resilience and opportunity. For many of our young athletes, it's the bridge between potential and possibility — a chance to represent their community, earn scholarships and even play professionally.

Our academy serves over 300 young people from low-income families in and around Kajiado. We provide free football training, life skills workshops, academic support and mentorship — all driven by volunteers who believe in the transformative power of sport.

Our Foundation

Mission · Vision · Strategy

Mission
To nurture young talent through world-class football training, education & mentorship — creating pathways to success both on and off the pitch.
Vision
A future where every child in Kajiado has equal access to quality sports education, inspiring a generation of confident, disciplined leaders.
Strategy
Community partnerships, donor support & volunteerism — building sustainable programmes that grow with our athletes and amplify local impact.

What We Do

Our Key Objectives

⚽
Elite Training
Provide world-class football coaching and development programmes for youth aged 6-18, led by CAF-licensed coaches and international volunteers.
🎓
Education First
Integrate academic support, life skills workshops and mentorship to ensure our athletes excel both on the pitch and in the classroom.
🏆
Competitive Excellence
Participate in regional and national tournaments, giving players exposure to scouts, clubs and scholarship opportunities.
🤝
Community Impact
Host community events, health camps and youth empowerment initiatives that extend far beyond the football field.
🌍
Global Partnerships
Build strategic alliances with international academies, NGOs and sponsors to create pathways for our athletes to play abroad.
💪
Holistic Development
Develop well-rounded individuals with strong character, discipline, leadership and the resilience to overcome any challenge.
